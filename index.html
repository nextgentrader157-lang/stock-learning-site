📚 Learning Articles

1. What is Share Market?

The share market is a place where investors buy and sell shares of companies to earn profit.

2. What is Intraday Trading?

Intraday trading involves buying and selling stocks within the same day.

3. What is Swing Trading?

Swing trading means holding stocks for a few days to capture short-term trends.

4. What is Long-term Investing?

Long-term investing focuses on holding stocks for years to build wealth.

5. What is Technical Analysis?

Technical analysis studies charts and indicators to predict price movement.

6. What is RSI Indicator?

RSI helps identify whether a stock is overbought or oversold.

7. What is MACD Indicator?

MACD shows trend direction and momentum in stock prices.

8. What is PE Ratio?

PE ratio helps determine stock valuation.

9. What is Stop Loss?

Stop loss limits your loss by exiting a trade at a fixed level.

10. What is Risk Management?

Risk management protects your capital and ensures long-term survival in trading.
