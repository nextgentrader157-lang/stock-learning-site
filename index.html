Basics of Share Market

What is Share Market?

The share market is a place where people buy and sell shares of companies. It helps companies raise money and investors earn profits.

What is NSE & BSE?

NSE (National Stock Exchange) and BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange) are the main stock exchanges in India where trading happens.

What is Demat Account?

A Demat account stores your shares in digital form. You need it to invest in stocks.
